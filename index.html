1 · Import your music

When enabled, Playlist Remixer checks Spotify first (if you have connected it). Apple’s public music catalog is used only when Spotify does not return a confident match. If neither finds a confident match, it uses the song with the most similar title and marks it as a “closest title” guess. Only the title and artist (and ISRC if available) of the track being looked up are sent to the providers actually used for that lookup. Existing values are never overwritten automatically.

How do I get my data out of each service?
  • Spotify: export a playlist as CSV with a tool like Exportify (it also adds tempo and energy), or request your data under Account → Privacy.
  • Apple Music: in the Music app on a computer, select a playlist and use File → Library → Export Playlist.
  • YouTube Music: Google Takeout can export your playlists as CSV.
  • Amazon Music & Pandora: no official export that I know of. Paste a list by hand, or use a transfer service like Soundiiz or TuneMyMusic to export to CSV first.

CSV columns I look for: track/title, artist, genre, playlist, play count, tempo/BPM, energy, valence, key, mode, producer, record label, release date.

Which of my tracks do I have files for?

Pick the folder where your music files live. Only file names are read, here in your browser: nothing is uploaded and no audio is opened. Tracks are matched by title (and artist, when it shows in the file or folder name). Your library is not changed.

Your library

Online metadata lookup can fill missing album details and artwork without changing information you already imported.

“Clear library” erases the working library. Your backups, saved looks and backgrounds are not affected.

Your library is saved in this browser only (“Clear library” erases it). The playlist tools do not upload your library. When automatic online metadata lookup is enabled, only the title, artist and available ISRC of tracks being looked up are sent to Apple’s public music catalog and, when you have already connected Spotify, Spotify’s catalog as a fallback; playlist names, the rest of your library and your account information are not sent by metadata lookup. When you choose to connect a service in “Transfer music”, the tracks of the playlist you pick are sent to that service for search and create/add requests. Connecting needs this page to be served over https; from a downloaded file everything else works offline. In the playlist tools, “better” means ranking, de-duplicating and re-sequencing the music you already have, plus pointing you to artists worth exploring. Use the export buttons, then import the result with Soundiiz or TuneMyMusic, or open the S/A/Z/P/Y links to find each song in a service.
